# Age suitability

**Bike Brevard Map**
Last updated 25 September 2026

This page exists because the App Store age rating questionnaire asks about
alcohol references, and this app has a "Breweries" category. It explains what
that category actually is, for anyone deciding whether the app is appropriate
for a family.

## What the app is

A map of Brevard, North Carolina's bike path and the places worth riding to
along it, plus nine curated rides with stop-by-stop notes. It is aimed at
families and visitors riding around a small town.

## What the brewery category contains

Ten destinations, each listed the same way every other destination is: a name,
a street address, a one-line description, opening hours and how far along the
path it sits.

Worth knowing about them:

- **Four are bike shops first.** Squatch Bikes & Brews, Weevil Outdoor Supply
  and The Hub & Pisgah Tavern appear in both the bike shop and brewery
  categories, because in Brevard that is genuinely what they are — you can get
  a tube changed and a coffee in the same building. Filtering the map to bike
  shops shows them too.
- **Most serve food and are general-audience venues.** Several have kitchens,
  lawns and outdoor seating; one hosts film screenings. They are the kind of
  place a family stops at mid-ride, and several host community and school
  fundraising events.
- **One is a bottle shop and wine bar**, and two of the ten are listed for
  wine as much as beer.

## What the app does not do

- No photographs of alcohol, and no depictions of anyone drinking. Every
  brewery is drawn as the same small map pin as every other category.
- No advertising, sponsorship or promotion. No business paid to be included and
  none can. The list is one person's judgement about what is worth riding to.
- Nothing can be bought, ordered or reserved in the app. There is no link out
  to any venue, no menu and no prices.
- No age gate, because there is nothing to gate — the app has no accounts, no
  user-generated content and no way to communicate with anyone.
- No encouragement to drink and ride. The category exists because taprooms are
  where the bike racks are in this town.

## Turning the category off

Categories are off by default. The app opens showing only the path, and a rider
chooses which categories to switch on. A family that does not want breweries on
the map simply leaves that toggle off, and the category never appears.
