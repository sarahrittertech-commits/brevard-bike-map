# Privacy policy

**Bike Brevard Map**
Last updated 16 October 2026

## The short version

Bike Brevard Map collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reporting and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the
app is recorded or transmitted anywhere.

## Your location

The app can show where you are on the map, as a dot, if you ask it to. It is
off until you tap the location button on the map. The first tap explains what
the dot is for and lets you say no, then your phone asks for permission. Tap
the button again and the dot goes away. Once you have allowed it, the dot
comes back on by itself each time you open the app; to stop that, choose
Never or Ask Next Time for the app in your phone's location settings.

- The app asks only for permission **while you are using it**. It never asks
  for, and cannot use, your location in the background.
- Your location is used for two things, both on your phone: drawing the dot,
  and working out how far a place is from you along the bike path ("Ride to
  here"). While the button is on, the app follows your position so that
  distance stays current; it stops the moment you turn the button off. Your
  location is never stored, logged, sent to us or sent anywhere else, and it
  is forgotten when you close the app.
- If you say no, everything else in the app works exactly as before. You can
  change your answer at any time in your phone's Settings.

## What the app stores

Every map, place, landmark and adventure in the app is bundled into the download
itself. The app reads that data from your device and never asks a server for it,
which is also why it works with no signal.

The app keeps no history of what you tap, search, filter or ride. It writes no
profile, no preferences and no usage log, on your device or anywhere else.

## What the app does not do

- It does not track you. Your location is never recorded, kept or sent
  anywhere, and the app never asks for it until you tap the location button.
- It has no accounts, sign-in or sign-up of any kind.
- It contains no advertising and no advertising or tracking identifiers.
- It contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.
- It makes no network requests of its own.
- It has no comments, reviews, uploads or any other way to submit content.

## Maps

The street map underneath the path is drawn by the phone's own map provider.
The app passes it no information about you — it asks only for the map of
Brevard, North Carolina.

**On iPhone** that is Apple Maps, which is part of iOS rather than part of this
app. Apple's handling of map data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy, not
this one.

**On Android** that is Google Maps, through Google's Maps SDK, which is built
into the app. Google says the SDK sends Google technical information on its own
account: the device model and operating system version, crash reports, the IP
address, a pseudonymous identifier used to count users of the SDK, and when the
map is panned or zoomed. This app does not see, receive or store any of it, and
the app passes the SDK no location of its own. When you turn on the location
dot, the dot is drawn by the Maps SDK from your phone's location, so Google's
handling of it is covered by Google's policy.
Google's handling of it is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not this one.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will say so
before that version is released, and the change will be listed in the
[release notes](./release-notes).
